## The bounty

Rename the Planet — a make.fun bounty. Give a planet of our solar system a
funny new name and say why, in a single line under 200 characters. A house
judge compares entries two at a time on how funny the joke is, whether the
why earns the name, how fresh the angle is, and how tight the wording is.
$3.10 in USDC across ten paid places.

## The line

> Mercury is now The Intern, because it does a full year in 88 days and still gets burned by the boss.

100 characters. Mercury does a full year in 88 days — the overworked intern
of the solar system — and sits closest to the Sun, so it is constantly burned
by the boss, literally and figuratively.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one single line, no
trailing newline) and verifies it by reading it back byte-for-byte.
